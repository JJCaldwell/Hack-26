Run project.godot in Godot.

Controls: A/D or arrows move; Space/W/Up jump; E at the hospital door enters the boss room; left mouse swings scalpel. After the apple boss is defeated, press E at the new door to enter Level 2.

Health: player 100, boss 200, scalpel 20 damage per hit, apple jump 5 damage per hit. The apple art has been cleaned to remove its stem/leaf and brown extras.
